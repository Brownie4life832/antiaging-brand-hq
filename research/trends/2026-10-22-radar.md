## ⚡ Flagged

- **Audit of 633 skincare brands: 47% had no independently verifiable evidence for any of their high-impact claims (M11 "Trust Gap in Skincare" report, covered by Dermatology Times Oct 13).** M11 checked 1,470 claims against each brand's product pages and other public materials. Only **39** had the proof on the page. **849** had evidence that existed somewhere but wasn't published where the claim was made. **454** had no published proof or were contradicted by the page, and **128** used regulated (drug-type) wording. M11 says a flagged claim isn't necessarily false, only unverifiable from public information. It frames this around **AI assistants**, which increasingly answer shoppers' "does this work?" questions from whatever brands publish. [Dermatology Times](https://www.dermatologytimes.com/view/ai-era-skincare-report-finds-widespread-gaps-in-verifiable-product-claims)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** "Show the proof next to the claim" is now a gap you can measure, and very few brands do it. Publishing the evidence on the page (study design, n, endpoints, where each claim comes from) would both build trust and make us legible to AI search. This fits our claims discipline and the public research-explorer site. **Action to consider:** a site/PDP standard where every efficacy claim links to its substantiation. It also shows **regulated wording** is being audited systematically, so treat words like "repair," "treat" and "reverse" with care. This was not caught on 10-13/10-15 because the item is from Oct 13 and only surfaced this run.

- **Niagen Bioscience makes topical NAD+ a permanent Tru Niagen line (Oct 20, 2026).** NanoCloud is a **waterless single-dose sachet** of patented nicotinamide riboside (NR) with HA/pullulan, squalane and vitamin E. Users stir it into their own water-based serum or moisturizer. It moves from a March 2026 limited "Innovation Lab" release to a core product, sold DTC on truniagen.com (~$59 / 30 sachets at the limited release; current price not verified). Supporting data is a **company-reported 6-week perception study of 37 people**: 73% agreed skin "appeared smoother," and 70% said it "looked more youthful" within 4 weeks. CEO Rob Fried calls it the entry point to "a large new market." [Stockhouse (Business Wire release)](https://stockhouse.com/news/press-releases/2026/10/20/niagen-bioscience-makes-skincare-a-permanent-tru-niagen-xae-category-launching) · [CosmeticsDesign-Europe (March launch)](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2026/03/17/waterless-topical-nad-inside-niagens-nanocloud-launch) · [Business Wire (March release)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260310114165/en/Niagen-Bioscience-Launches-Niagen%C2%AE-Skincare-Innovation-Lab-and-Debuts-Dermatologist-Tested-Niagen-NanoCloud%E2%84%A2-From-Tru-Niagen-Powered-by-Patented-Niagen%C2%AE/)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** (1) Another **supplement-native longevity company moving into topicals** with "ingestible + topical" pairing, after Timeline/Mitopure → Lancôme. The longevity-topicals lane is getting crowded from the supplement side, not just from beauty majors. (2) The **stability-first format** (waterless, dose-on-demand) is a real formulation answer to unstable actives. Worth raising with our formulator for any NAD+-precursor or vitamin C ideas, and it is relevant to the refillable/lean-routine logic. (3) Evidence is **perception-only**, so this is not a bar we'd need to beat on clinicals. Our formulator's custom-delivery work plus instrumental endpoints could out-evidence it.

## Ingredients & Science

- **Topical NAD+ precursors (NR):** see Flagged. There is also a registered trial of *topical nicotinamide riboside on signs of facial aging* (NCT07774000). Results aren't posted. Track it as the first independent-ish human data on topical NR. [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07774000)
- **"Skin longevity" evidence pushback from dermatologists.** Dermatology Times ran an opinion piece titled "Skin Longevity Needs Evidence, Not Hype." It uses topical rapamycin as an example: the small exploratory RCT is "provocative" but does not establish it as a proven skin-longevity treatment. ReachMD proposes ranking longevity interventions into evidence tiers, with topical retinoids and antioxidants in the top tier. *Exact publication date of the Dermatology Times piece not confirmed.* [Dermatology Times](https://www.dermatologytimes.com/view/skin-longevity-needs-evidence-not-hype) · [ReachMD](https://reachmd.com/programs/modern-aesthetics/the-longevity-question-we-should-be-asking/57929/) · [Rapamycin RCT (2019, background)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6925069)
  - **Read:** derms are starting to police "longevity" language, which supports positioning on **well-evidenced actives + own data** over buzz mechanisms.
- **Peptide meta-analysis:** the *Frontiers in Medicine* review was updated again (Oct 16). It reports significant gains in hydration and brightness, especially for **oral** peptides, and only a **modest** pooled effect on wrinkles. That's consistent with our 10-13/10-15 read. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1618306/full)

## Regulatory

- **EU Omnibus VI:** still **not formally adopted** as of Oct 22. The provisional deal is from June 17, and the Council aims for adoption "in the course of 2026." It includes differentiated CMR phase-out periods, **prior notification of nanomaterials**, digital labels on small inner packs, and a 1 Jan 2030 application date for the new labelling rules. The nanomaterial notification matters if our formulator's advanced-delivery systems use nano-scale carriers. [EU Transition Pathways](https://transition-pathways.europa.eu/chemicals/regulatory-roadmap/co-legislators-agreement-simplify-chemical-labelling-cosmetics-and) · [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/hazardous-or-safe-unpacking-the-e-us-omnibus-vi)
- **US / MoCRA:** no new FDA rules, guidance or cosmetic warning letters found in-window. GMP and fragrance-allergen labeling rules remain pending, and the GMP rule is reportedly a "long-term action" that won't be finalized in 2026. The **<$1M US sales small-business exemption** from registration/listing is worth noting for the launch phase (verify against the statute). [AestheticMedGuide](https://aestheticmedguide.com/blog/mocra-deadlines-2026-pending-fda-cosmetic-rules) · [Dicentra](https://dicentra.com/blog/adverse-reaction-reporting/mocra-in-2026-what-has-changed-and-what-does-fda-expect-from-the-cosmetics-industry)
- **FTC / NAD:** no skincare advertising decisions found in-window. The most recent relevant NAD precedent is still **PrettyBoy (Mar 2026)**: a certification without supporting testing was not enough for visual claims, and NAD recommended pulling before/after photos. [CosmeticsDesign](https://www.cosmeticsdesign.com/Article/2026/03/12/nad-questions-prettyboys-skincare-claims-and-advertising-tactics/)
