## ⚡ Flagged

- **La Mer has relaunched its icon serum, The Concentrate, as a "barrier serum" (rolling out fall 2026; first trade listings appeared late Aug as "coming soon").** The new formula uses "4x concentrated Miracle Broth™" under a new "Bio-Regenerative Skin Intelligence™" platform. It is positioned to calm visible sensitivity and "strengthen the skin's natural barrier." The brand claims the barrier is "strengthened by 38%" after one application and that "90%" saw fewer-looking fine lines at 4 weeks. Sizes are 30 ml and 50 ml, and US pricing isn't confirmed yet. [Ebony launch roundup](https://www.ebony.com/from-cecreds-new-fragrance-to-la-mers-new-concentrate-these-are-the-latest-beauty-launches-this-week/) · [Dealmoon pre-launch listing](https://www.dealmoon.com/en/unlock-a-new-era-of-skin-coming-soon-la-mer-the-concentrate-4x-concentrated/6095854.html) · [Breuninger listing](https://www.breuninger.com/se/en/b/la-mer/the-concentrate/1003426726/p/)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** together with Paula's Choice ($49, 10-01 radar), this means **both the masstige and the ultra-luxury ends now market "barrier repair" as an anti-aging hero.** Our planned premium barrier-repair cream is no longer entering an empty lane. Instead it sits **between** a $49 Sephora option and a $300+ heritage-luxury serum. Its differentiation has to be **evidence quality** (instrumental TEWL/corneometry on the finished formula, not "after one application" perception claims), custom delivery, and refill economics. Also worth noting: La Mer uses a proprietary-sounding "technology" name. Our claims notes should decide whether we coin platform names at all and how we substantiate them.

- **Scarlett Johansson's sensitive-skin brand The Outset shut down (announced Sep 28, 2026) after four years,** despite celebrity backing and Sephora/Nordstrom/Goop distribution. The website has stopped taking orders. [Business of Fashion](https://www.businessoffashion.com/news/beauty/the-outset-closing-scarlett-johansson/) · [Happi](https://www.happi.com/breaking-news/skincare-brand-the-outset-shutters-operations/) · [NBC Select](https://www.nbcnews.com/select/shopping/the-outset-closing-sensitive-skin-alternatives-rcna600568)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** this is a cautionary data point for the "gentle / clean / sensitive-skin" lane. Fame plus wide retail wasn't enough without a defensible product or evidence story. It supports the plan to **invest in substantiation and a lean, hero-led range** rather than buying awareness. It is also mild evidence that "clean + gentle" on its own is a crowded positioning. That's relevant to the open positioning options in [`brand/positioning.md`](../../brand/positioning.md), but it doesn't decide anything.

## Ingredients & Science

- **A critical review in *Biochemical Pharmacology* (Gürtler et al., published Jul 29, 2026; surfaced in coverage this week) asks whether skin aging can be reversed.** It finds **topical retinoids have the most consistent evidence** (tolerability is still the limit). Evidence for antioxidants, hyaluronic acid, collagen peptides, and hormones is described as "heterogeneous or inconclusive." It also highlights recurring weaknesses in the field's studies: small samples, short durations, subjective endpoints, weak controls, and heavy industry sponsorship. [Skin Aging & Challenges summary](https://skin-challenges.com/can-skin-aging-be-reversed-new-scientific-review-questions-the-evidence/) · [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006295226002315)
  - **Read:** this is a ready-made outline for our own **evidence bar**. It lists exactly the study-design flaws our finished-product testing should avoid, and it is a useful citation for claims discipline in [`research/science/`](../science/). It doesn't change the ingredient shortlist, but it reinforces that retinoids are the evidence anchor and that HA and collagen-peptide hero claims are weak.

## Regulatory

- **Australia (ACCC) microplastics enforcement:** stop notices went to Mecca, Sephora, and ASOS over 12 exfoliating products that contain microplastics, with fines reported from AU$550k. Relevant if we ever sell in AU. Keep any exfoliant or texture system free of microbeads/intentionally added microplastics, which is also consistent with the EU REACH microplastics restriction. [British Beauty Blogger](https://britbeautyblog.substack.com/p/beauty-business-news-october-2026) *(single-source secondary report; verify against ACCC before relying on it)*
- **Transdermal-claim enforcement (tangential):** Kind Patches faces a US class action (Sep 2026) over dopamine/serotonin "mood" patch claims, following a UK ASA ruling in Feb 2026 that found the claims unsubstantiated. It's a reminder that claiming an active is **delivered through the skin** needs delivery data at the dose in the product, which is directly relevant to any "advanced delivery" story from our formulator. [ASA ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/kind-patches-ltd-a26-1333913-kind-patches-ltd.html) · [Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/kind-patches-class-action-claims-dopamine-patches-do-not-work-as-advertised/)
- **EU Omnibus VI** is still expected to be formally adopted in October (already tracked, and no adoption confirmed in-window). **EU PFAS cutoff Oct 10** is still pending (see 10-01). [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/hazardous-or-safe-unpacking-the-e-us-omnibus-vi)
- No new FDA cosmetic warning letters or MoCRA rules found in-window. The GMP rule remains a long-term action and won't be final in 2026. [Registrar Corp](https://www.registrarcorp.com/blog/cosmetics/mocra/mocra-unified-agenda/)
