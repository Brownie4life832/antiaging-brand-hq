## ⚡ Flagged

- **Paula's Choice, a tracked competitor, launched a $49 peptide + barrier-lipid moisturizer with a celebrity campaign (~Sep 15, 2026).** The Pro-Collagen Peptide Plumping Intensive Moisturizer combines their "3x Pro-Collagen Peptide Complex" with red micro-algae extract and a "moisture-locking lipid technology designed to reinforce the skin barrier." The brand claims "up to 120 hours" of hydration. It's their 6th peptide launch and 2nd moisturizer. It sells at Sephora (in-store and online), Amazon, and PaulasChoice.com, with Hannah Waddingham fronting a global campaign. [Drug Store News](https://drugstorenews.com/paulas-choice-launches-new-moisturizer-campaign-starring-hannah-waddingham) · [NewBeauty](https://www.newbeauty.com/view/hannah-waddingham-paulas-choice-moisturizer) · [The Carousel](https://thecarousel.com/beauty-fashion/hannah-waddingham-wants-you-to-stop-apologising-for-being-too-much/)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** "peptide + barrier lipids in a rich cream" is now available at **$49 in Sephora** from an evidence-forward brand. That's the masstige floor under our planned premium barrier-repair cream. A premium price will have to be justified by something Paula's Choice can't match at $49: **custom delivery, clinical substantiation on the finished formula, and refill economics**. "Has peptides and ceramides" is no longer a differentiator. Note their marketing approach too: they cast a 50+ celebrity and frame it as "intensity is a strength," which sits close to some of the open positioning options in [`brand/positioning.md`](../../brand/positioning.md). That's one more data point for that open decision, not a recommendation.

- **FDA clarified on Sep 9, 2026 that it issues no certificates or letters verifying cosmetic facility registration or product listing.** A registration or listing number "does not indicate FDA approval." The clarification was prompted by e-commerce platforms (notably **TikTok Shop**) asking sellers for "proof of FDA registration." [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-news-events/fda-clarifies-it-does-not-provide-certificates-or-other-documents-verify-compliance-cosmetic-product) · [ArentFox Schiff](https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/alerts/read-my-lipstick-fda-will-not-certify-cosmetic-facility-registration-or-product) · [Happi](https://www.happi.com/breaking-news/fda-clarifies-certificates-facility-registration/)
  - **Why it matters for Kintsugi:** this is a direct **claims guardrail**. We should never write "FDA registered/approved/certified" on packs, site, or ads in a way that implies endorsement. If we sell through TikTok Shop or similar channels, plan to show **our own registration/listing numbers and the contract manufacturer's MoCRA records**, not an FDA certificate, because no such certificate exists. We should add this to the claims-discipline notes.

## Ingredients & Science

- **A *Science* perspective (JAX / UMass Chan / Duke, Sep 2, 2026) argues aged skin immunity is "recalibrated," not just weaker.** It says skin-microbiome shifts may track **frailty** better than chronological age, and it proposes microneedle-patch sampling as a non-biopsy biomarker readout. **Caveats:** this is an opinion piece built on observational and correlational evidence, with no intervention data. **Read:** it gives scientific backing to framing barrier and skin-immune health as part of "skin longevity." It may also be a future citation for a microbiome-aware formula. It does **not** support any efficacy claim. [Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-09-skin-reveal-clues-aging-frailty.html) · DOI 10.1126/science.aeh4348
- **Aesthetic-exosome market report (GlobeNewswire, Sep 29, 2026).** It sizes the market at ~$39.7M in 2025, growing to a projected $139.8M by 2033 (18% CAGR), mostly in-clinic and post-procedure. This is a paid market-research press release, so treat the figures as low-confidence. It doesn't change our research-open status on exosomes. The market is small, mostly in clinics, and still has a regulatory ceiling in the US. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/29/3370570/28124/en/aesthetic-exosomes-market-size-share-trends-and-growth-forecast-2026-2033-market-to-reach-usd-139-8-million-as-regenerative-aesthetics-drives-18-0-cagr.html)
