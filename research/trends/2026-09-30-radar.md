## Ingredients & Science

- **Topical senolytic ABT-263 (navitoclax) improves wound healing in aged mouse skin.** ScienceDaily ran it Sep 23, 2026. Boston University: 5 days of topical dosing reduced senescent-cell burden, and 80% of treated aged mice had fully healed wounds by day 24 vs. 56% of untreated. Collagen and angiogenesis genes were upregulated, with a transient inflammatory spike. **Caveats:** mice only, no human data. ABT-263 is a **drug** (Bcl-2 inhibitor), not a cosmetic ingredient. The underlying paper is Shvedova et al., *Aging* vol. 17, so this is press recirculation of an already-published study, not a new result. **Read:** it gives mechanistic support to the senolytic narrative the Flagged launches are borrowing. It does **not** validate cosmetic "senolytics" like fisetin at over-the-counter doses. [ScienceDaily (Sep 23, 2026)](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260922223008.htm) · [Shvedova et al., *Aging* — DOI 10.18632/aging.206165](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206165)

- **Integrative "in-and-out" longevity dermatology review (Springer, *Dermatology and Therapy*, 2026).** It frames topicals (retinoids, peptides, antioxidants, exosome formulations) alongside oral nutraceuticals. This is a narrative review, not new trial data. It is useful as a citable framing source if we ever consider a supplement-adjacent line, which is **not** under consideration. [Dermatology and Therapy — Integrative Dermatology for Longevity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13555-026-01732-y)

- **SIRT6 is being pushed as "the new SIRT1" in ingredient-supplier trend copy.** This is supplier marketing, not clinical evidence. Watch it as a coming buzzword, but don't act on it. [Grand Ingredients — Skin Longevity 2026: SIRT6](https://grandingredients.com/skin-longevity-2026/)

## Regulatory

- **FDA final order (Sep 10–11, 2026) removes PABA and trolamine salicylate from the OTC sunscreen monograph M020**, effective **Sep 11, 2027**, on safety grounds. FDA knows of no US products currently using either filter. This has no direct impact on Kintsugi, but it signals that FDA is actively modernizing the sunscreen monograph (alongside BEMT). [Federal Register 2026-18551](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-11/pdf/2026-18551.pdf) · [ArentFox Schiff](https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/alerts/there-goes-the-sunscreen-fda-removes-paba-and-trolamine-salicylate-otc)
- **EU PFAS cutoff for cosmetics is Oct 10, 2026, 10 days out.** This is the REACH transitional-period end flagged since 07-03. It only matters for us if any sourced base or packaging component carries PFAS, so confirm "PFAS-free" in supplier specs. [UL Solutions](https://www.ul.com/news/eu-sets-pfas-restrictions-consumer-products)
- **EU Omnibus VI chemicals package** is expected to be adopted in October and in law by year-end. It revises how CMR substances are handled in cosmetics and is broadly viewed as positive and long-term. Separately, on **Jul 8, 2026** the EU notified the WTO of a draft amendment to Reg. 1223/2009 combining Omnibus Ingredients III with CMR Omnibus IX, which brings new prohibitions and restrictions. Check it against any shortlisted INCI before we lock a formula. [BeautyMatter — Omnibus VI](https://beautymatter.com/articles/hazardous-or-safe-unpacking-the-e-us-omnibus-vi) · [REACH24H — 2026 EU updates](https://en.reach24h.com/news/insights/cosmetic/eu-cosmetic-regulatory-updates-2026)
- **MoCRA:** no new rule in-window. 2026 is the **first biennial facility-registration renewal cycle** for early-2024 registrants, and the fragrance-allergen disclosure rule is still pending. [Ecomundo — MoCRA 2026](https://ecomundo.eu/en/blog/mocra-update-2026)
