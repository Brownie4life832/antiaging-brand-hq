# Finalist decision pack — 2026-07-16

## Where the exercise landed

The fresh round generated **1,020 source candidates**, producing **989 unique names** after exact deduplication. Three independent naked-name curators advanced 151 names at least once; 62 earned two or more votes; 10 survived all three independent commercial, strategy, and verbal hard-gate rankings. Preliminary collision screening then removed the attractive-but-occupied names and examined backups from the consensus tier.

The result is not six equally endorsed names. It is:

1. **Restraint** — evidence leader and only preliminary-green name.
2. **Bearing** — ordered fallback and strongest pure brand word; preliminary amber.
3. Four controlled challengers that must prove the leaders wrong in a cold-buyer test: **Leeway, In Earnest, Byline, Actual**.

Three independent final adversarial reviews all selected **Restraint** as primary and **Bearing** as fallback after seeing the strategy, verbal risks, and preliminary collision findings.

## The recommendation before buyer testing

### Primary — Restraint

**15-second story:** Restraint is skincare disciplined enough to leave out what has not earned its place—from ingredients and unnecessary steps to inflated promises and age panic.

**Strategic advantage:** It converts the positioning into a governing principle: evidence over excess, disciplined judgment over trend-chasing, and adult self-possession over fear.

**Why it is memorable:** It has a useful negative edge. Most skincare promises more; Restraint says better judgment matters more than volume.

**Principal weakness accepted knowingly:** The naked word can imply repression, deprivation, physical coercion, joylessness, or withholding. That is not cosmetic. It is the one issue the buyer test must attack directly.

**Non-negotiable interpretation:** The brand restrains formulas, routines, claims, and commercial excess. It never restrains the customer, pleasure, expression, ambition, or age.

**Identity consequence:** The visual and verbal system must add warmth, tactility, and aliveness. A cold clinical-grey identity would turn productive tension into punishment.

**Preliminary screen:** Green only in the limited knockout sense. Professional clearance is still required.

### Automatic fallback — Bearing

**15-second story:** Bearing is care for how you carry yourself—composed, capable, and recognizably your own—supported by precise formulas rather than promises of youth.

**Strategic advantage:** “Her bearing” expresses mature presence and self-possession with natural premium weight.

**Principal weakness accepted knowingly:** Mechanical components dominate search and may create an industrial, technical, masculine, or nautical reading. An exact pet shampoo/lotion filing was abandoned in 2024 and Bearing-branded pet lotion remains in commerce.

**Identity consequence:** It needs an immediate skincare descriptor and must avoid engineering, compass, metallic, marine, and heavy masculine codes.

**Preliminary screen:** Amber. It becomes the selected name only if attorney-led clearance is acceptable.

## What could overturn the recommendation

Restraint remains the primary unless one of these occurs:

1. **Repeated damaging association:** at least two independent target buyers, unprompted, reach the same punitive/coercive/deprivation reading and still feel it after seeing the skincare context.
2. **Category rejection:** most testers believe the name is fundamentally wrong for a premium skincare company after the identical prototype is shown.
3. **Professional knockout:** an attorney finds a meaningful conflict or protectability problem not found in the preliminary screen.
4. **Founder non-commitment:** the founder cannot truthfully promise to build the “discipline without deprivation” interpretation for ten years. Discomfort alone is not enough; an actual strategic contradiction is.

If condition 1 or 2 occurs, test **Bearing** against the same gates. If condition 3 occurs after the decision, the recorded fallback activates automatically. Do not reopen generation.

## Final decision criteria

Once fatal gates are cleared, compare only the final three:

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to use |
|---|---:|---|
| Distinctiveness and memory | 40% | Unaided delayed recall, first-association specificity, competitor-regret test, visual/verbal salience. |
| Strategic leverage | 35% | Ability to encode the frozen position, create a useful brand point of view, and support a ten-year portfolio. |
| Fluency and verbal usability | 25% | Hear-to-spell, see-to-say, product syntax, recommendation sentence, podcast introduction. |

Clearance is a gate, not a weighted criterion. A red name cannot compensate with a high creative score.
