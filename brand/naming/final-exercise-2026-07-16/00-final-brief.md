# Final naming exercise — decision brief

> **Decision deadline:** 2026-07-16, 6:00 p.m. local time  
> **Status:** Final closed naming round. The prior corpus is evidence, not a shortlist.  
> **Decision owner:** Founder. Advisers and tests supply evidence; they do not get vetoes.

## Why this round is different

The previous work achieved enormous lexical volume but converged on one narrow aesthetic: soft foreign/Latinate words and metaphors of calm, shelter, repair, light, time, nature, stone, and craft. The brief also asked the name to remain neutral across incompatible positioning lanes. That produced many explainable names but no inevitable decision.

This round changes the hypothesis. It does not rerun the same search with more words.

## Frozen strategic assumption

Unless the founder explicitly replaces this before screening begins, use this:

> A premium, evidence-led skincare brand for adults roughly 35–55 who want visible improvement and long-term skin capability without being told to become younger. Barrier-first custom formulation and advanced delivery are the reason to believe; they are not the master-brand naming territory.

### The name's one primary job

Signal **self-possession and intelligent care**: a grown-up brand with a point of view, not another promise of rescue, reversal, purity, or eternal youth.

### The productive tension

- Credible, but not clinical.
- Alive, but not youth-coded.
- Premium, but not precious or spa-like.
- Distinctive, but immediately usable in speech.

### What the name should make a cold listener expect

- A modern, considered consumer brand.
- High-quality products and disciplined judgment.
- A brand comfortable speaking to adults as adults.

### What the name must not imply

- Fear of age or a promise to reverse it.
- A medical treatment, drug, or unsupported biological claim.
- One ingredient, one technology, or one hero SKU.
- Fragile luxury, passive protection, or mystical wellness.

## Fresh search territories

1. **Recognizable self** — likeness, character, expression, presence, continuity of identity.
2. **Sovereignty / on your terms** — agency, authorship, permission, choice, refusal, self-command.
3. **Discernment / fewer, better** — editing, selection, standards, signal over noise, what earns a place.
4. **Adaptive capacity** — range, reserve, latitude, readiness, responsiveness, recovery without defensive imagery.
5. **Sensory action / micro-gesture** — short, ownable words shaped by how application moves or feels.
6. **Mature aliveness / presence** — animation, warmth, expression, engagement, immediacy without glow/youth clichés.
7. **Competent care / no drama** — capability, usefulness, exactness, reliability, human confidence.
8. **Founder provenance** — only if a genuinely meaningful personal source is supplied; never invented biography.

## Mandatory exclusions

### Retired ideas

- Repair as mend, seam, kintsugi, gold, restoration, or wholeness.
- Protection as wall, shell, armor, fortress, haven, cocoon, veil, guard, or shelter.
- Calm/spa language: quiet, still, calm, rest, repose, solace, serenity, poise.
- Time/age proxies: forever, perennial, old growth, patina, seasons, dusk/dawn, moon/sun.
- Stone, tree, tide, sea, forest, bark, kiln, glaze, textile, or architecture metaphors.
- Mythology, celestial objects, botany, periodic-table styling, and foreign-dictionary treasure hunting.

### Retired word-building habits

- No pseudo-pharma `-ium`, `-on`, `-ene`, or similar scientific costume.
- No invented Romance-luxury names ending `-a`, `-ia`, `-ara`, `-ora`, `-ena`, `-eva`, `-ova`, `-elle`, or `-enne`.
- Block exhausted roots including `sol`, `vel`, `ver`, `ser`, `len`, `ten`, `cal`, `lun`, `aur`, `ae/aev`, `mir`, `vita/viv`, `cera`, `mend`, `quiet`, `still`, `stone`, `tide`, `keep`, `well`, `haven`, and `refug`.
- Reject any coinage with obvious interchangeable siblings.
- Reject any name whose value depends on “it means ___ in ___.”

## Preferred forms

- Bold, familiar real words used unexpectedly.
- Short stance phrases or English-native compounds.
- Crisp action-like coinages with an obvious pronunciation.
- House/surname-like forms that feel established without pretending to be a founder name.
- Unrelated metaphors from territories not mined previously.

## Generation rules

- Generate blind to prior scores and favorites.
- No persuasive rationales during generation.
- Tag territory and form only.
- Deduplicate exact and phonetic matches against the old corpus.
- Equalize output across territories and forms so one aesthetic cannot dominate.
- Stop permanently at the declared cutoff. No “one more batch” after screening begins.

## Success standard

The winner does not need to feel perfect. It must:

1. Pass every fatal usability and preliminary-risk gate.
2. Work as a naked word before a story is supplied.
3. Support the frozen strategic assumption.
4. Create more useful distinctiveness than the surviving alternatives.
5. Be a name the founder is willing to build consistently for ten years.
