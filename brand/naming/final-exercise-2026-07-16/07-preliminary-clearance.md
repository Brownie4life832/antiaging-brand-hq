# Preliminary collision screen — 2026-07-15

> **Purpose:** knockout triage before the 2026-07-16 decision session. This is not a legal opinion, a complete trademark search, or confirmation that any name is available.

## Standard used

The screen looked for exact and confusingly similar use in skincare, cosmetics, fragrance, personal care, oral care, aesthetics, supplements, wellness, and related retail/services. It considered live brands, accessible federal/international records, ordinary search ownership, and overall commercial impression.

The USPTO says a comprehensive clearance search must cover federally registered and pending marks, common-law/internet use, state registries, domains, and relevant international sources. It also asks whether marks are similar in sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression when used on related goods—not merely whether exact spellings match.

Status language in this document:

- **Preliminary green:** no obvious conflict surfaced in targeted searches. It still needs attorney-led clearance.
- **Amber:** no knockout was proved, but meaningful adjacent use, weak ownership, or unresolved evidence makes pursuit conditional.
- **Red:** direct or sufficiently close category evidence makes further pursuit imprudent.

## Binding legal rule

Tomorrow's output is a **preferred name plus an ordered fallback**, both contingent on professional clearance. Do not print packaging, announce publicly, purchase a large domain portfolio, or commission a full identity until a trademark attorney has searched and interpreted at least Classes 3, 5, 35, and 44 in the intended launch and expansion markets.
