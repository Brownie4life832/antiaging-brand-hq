# What the previous search taught us

## The short diagnosis

The old corpus failed through **convergence and decision design**, not lack of effort.

- The brand was asked to remain neutral across five positioning lanes. Strong names exclude territory; neutrality rewarded vagueness.
- The supposedly neutral brief still anchored on Kintsugi, repair, resilience, protection, calm, and time-as-ally.
- More than 5,000 candidates increased option volume without recording a founder rejection taxonomy or changing the hypothesis.
- Rich rationales encouraged evaluators to score the explanation rather than the naked name.
- Goldseam moving from #197 to #1 after recalibration showed that the rankings were highly frame-sensitive despite decimal precision.
- Clearance and real buyer usability arrived too late or not at all.
- “Polarization” was sometimes treated as proof of power when it could equally indicate obscurity or poor fluency.

## Corpus concentration

Analysis of the Top 500 found:

- **42.2%** end in `a`.
- **26.4%** begin with only 11 favored root families.
- **43.8%** invoke calm, quiet, stillness, rest, or serenity.
- **21.8%** invoke protection, shelter, or barrier.
- **20.0%** invoke repair, renewal, or wholeness.
- **17.0%** invoke light, glow, moon, or sun.
- **14.8%** invoke time or endurance.
- **12.0%** invoke stone, tree, tide, or nature.

Even the “full 17-strategy” survivor pool was uneven: intangible-to-tangible, world-language, periodic-table, and morpheme-engine outputs dominated, while no survivor represented “reframe the question/name the future” or “name the audacity/worldview.”

## What is retired

The following celebrated paths are excluded as controls, not treated as finalists:

- **Goldseam / Seramend / Slowmend / Mendwell** — hard-lock repair and revive the Kintsugi anchor.
- **Bield / Abri / Refugia / Carapace / Tutela** — repeat passive-protection territory and often add pronunciation or translation friction.
- **Celadon / Tenmoku / Wabi / Solera / Burl** — return to “age becomes beautiful through craft/nature” and need an essay.
- **Duramen / Anneal / Ashlar** — repeat obscure material-science metaphors and can feel cold or masculine.

These may serve as comparison controls in testing, but they receive no legacy advantage and do not enter fresh generation.
