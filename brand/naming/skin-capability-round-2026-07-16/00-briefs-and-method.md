# Skin capability naming round

> **Status:** Active generation brief, 2026-07-16  
> **Decision owner:** Founder  
> **Launch category:** Premium evidence-led anti-aging skincare in the United States  
> **Purpose:** Explore three related but strategically distinct worlds through David Placek's 17 generation moves without recycling the project's exhausted aesthetics.

## Shared brand truth

The brand helps adults improve how their skin functions, responds, and appears over time. It explains the formulation logic and evidence without asking customers to accept opaque authority. The brand does not promise youth, reversal, perfection, or medical treatment.

## Founder-taste controls

- Prefer an immediate, graspable idea over an etymology lesson.
- Prefer category relevance, a familiar word with one legible modification, authentic insider terminology, a direct proposition, or a productive contradiction.
- A name must work before its rationale is supplied.
- Avoid soft foreign/Latinate luxury, pseudo-pharma construction, mythology, spa calm, repair/protection imagery, and generic proof/editorial language.
- Do not force a candidate from an exercise merely to complete a quota.
- `Skin Training`, `Skin Practice`, and `Personal Baseline` are briefing labels, not presumed name candidates.

## World A — Skin Training

### Reframe

Anti-aging is not reversal. It is progressive conditioning: helping skin retain or improve useful capacity through consistent, intelligent inputs.

### Customer behavior

The customer expects a program rather than a miracle, understands why consistency matters, and judges progress over time without chasing a universal younger ideal.

### Productive tension

- Capable, not aggressive.
- Progressive, not punishing.
- Expert, not gym-bro.
- Active, not youth-coded.

### Source worlds

Coaching, athletic conditioning, movement science, progressive training, physical therapy, skill acquisition, performance testing, and authentic training-session language.

### Avoid

`gym`, `fit`, `muscle`, `beast`, `peak`, `gains`, body-transformation clichés, and claims that cosmetics literally train cells or alter physiology without substantiation.

## World B — Skin Practice

### Reframe

Advanced skincare becomes useful when knowledge turns into repeatable technique. The product is part of an informed practice, not a collectible shelf of promises.

### Customer behavior

The customer learns, applies, observes, adjusts, and becomes more capable of making skincare decisions.

### Productive tension

- Consistent, not joyless.
- Skilled, not teacherly.
- Ritualized, not mystical.
- Informed, not academic.

### Source worlds

Deliberate practice, rehearsal, studio technique, coaching cues, apprenticeship, notation, routines, feedback, and practical mastery.

### Avoid

Generic `ritual`, clinic/professional-services language, schoolroom language, moralized discipline, and ordinary package instructions that may fail to function as marks.

## World C — Personal Baseline

### Reframe

The relevant comparison is not a younger stranger or an average consumer. It is the customer's own skin, starting point, response, and progress.

### Customer behavior

The customer expects personalization, honest measurement, and improvement judged against an individual reference rather than a universal beauty ideal.

### Productive tension

- Measured, not clinical.
- Personal, not sentimental.
- Specific, not narrow.
- Better, without ranking people.

### Source worlds

N-of-1 research, within-subject comparison, benchmarking, reference standards, calibration, longitudinal observation, tailoring/fit, and individual-response design.

### Avoid

Diagnostic or medical implications, numerical efficacy claims, body-score language, universal optimization, and constructions that imply the customer is defective.

## Placek exercise matrix

The round uses the complete 17-move catalog as a set of lenses, not 17 equal survivor quotas.

| # | Move | Application in this round |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Make the intangible tangible | Turn capability, consistency, and self-relative progress into physical scenes and actions. |
| 2 | Benefit ladder / ultimate feeling | Climb from formula to function to the customer's lived confidence. |
| 3 | Reframe the question / name the future | Name what skincare becomes after rescue/reversal language is removed. |
| 4 | Anti-descriptor | Ban anti-aging, youth, repair, glow, clinical, bio, and lab. |
| 5 | Win by contrast | Source from training, studio practice, and N-of-1 systems rather than beauty language. |
| 6 | Name the audacity / worldview | State the refusal of universal age ideals and miracle marketing. |
| 7 | Deconstruct / reconstruct | Build action-like words from authentic motions and cues. |
| 8 | Portmanteau | Fuse only when both source words remain legible and the seam is clean. |
| 9 | Morpheme engine | Use English-native fragments; reject interchangeable luxury siblings. |
| 10 | Periodic-table/scientific borrowing | Negative-control pass only; no forced survivor because the aesthetic is retired. |
| 11 | Sound-first coinage | Favor clear pronunciation and capable, human energy. |
| 12 | Structural/orthographic devices | Test one legible omission, compression, symmetry, or joined phrase. |
| 13 | Compounds | Seek a third idea from two familiar words, with category relevance where possible. |
| 14 | Real-word metaphor/eponym | Use authentic terms that perform strategic work without an explanation lecture. |
| 15 | World-language hunt | Negative-control pass only; foreign-dictionary naming is retired by founder taste. |
| 16 | Synchronicity/source-library raid | Raid coaching, movement science, studio practice, metrology, and N-of-1 research. |
| 17 | Three-team decoy | Generate as a conditioning system, a mastery studio, and an individualized measurement service before revealing skincare. |

## Funnel

1. Generate raw candidates without scoring or availability bias.
2. Normalize and deduplicate against the retained naming corpus.
3. Curate naked names for immediate idea, fluency, category/product syntax, and founder-taste fit.
4. Apply fatal claim, damaging-association, explanation-dependence, and headroom gates.
5. Run a narrow local federal exact/one-edit screen and a current marketplace exact-name scan on the smallest serious set.
6. Present no more than four survivors per world, with one-sentence stories and explicit liabilities.

Preliminary screening is not legal clearance. Finalists require attorney-led searching before adoption or launch spend.
