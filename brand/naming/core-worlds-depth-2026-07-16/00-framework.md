# Core worlds depth round

**Status:** Active naming program  
**Date:** July 16, 2026  
**Category:** Premium, evidence-led anti-aging skincare in the United States

## Objective

Return to the brand's durable strategic worlds, deepen their source language, and run every world through the complete 17-exercise Placek-style generation catalog. Personalization / individual fit is excluded by founder direction.

The exercise is designed to produce names, not explanations. Strategic fit cannot rescue a weak naked name.

## Revised world order

1. Time and Skin Longevity
2. Nothing Hidden and Transparency
3. Bare Face and Recognizable Self
4. Skin Capability and Training
5. Visible Performance
6. Barrier Strength and Adaptive Capacity
7. Evidence and Proof
8. Better Informed and Customer Agency

Transparency moves from seventh to second by founder direction. The rank controls generation priority and the small tie-break applied during rating; it does not guarantee representation in the final slate.

Each world can use any form below. Forms may overlap.

1. Direct conceptual real word
2. Category-relevant compound
3. Transparent spelling modification
4. Authentic insider terminology
5. Attribute or philosophy plus category
6. Explicit commercial proposition
7. Bold contradiction
8. Familiar idiom with a category-specific second meaning
9. Unexpected real word or metaphor
10. Short stance phrase
11. Verb, action, or command
12. English-native compound or compression
13. Clean portmanteau with legible sources
14. System, method, standard, club, or institution name
15. Meaningful number, notation, or code
16. Authentic object, tool, gesture, or process
17. Crisp sound-led coinage without luxury or pharma mimicry
18. House, surname, or character-like form without fabricated provenance

## Rating model

Every project-fresh candidate is rated from 1-10 on:

- Naked brand character
- Founder-form fit
- World truth
- Category ignition
- Distinctiveness
- Fluency
- Bottle and verbal usability
- Story headroom

Penalties are applied for campaign-like length, generic informational language, retired naming aesthetics, awkward pronunciation, explanation dependence, likely failure-to-function wording, and obvious category cliches. Ratings are a structured creative triage tool, not consumer research.

## Funnel

1. Generate across every world-exercise cell.
2. Normalize and deduplicate internally and against the historical project corpus.
3. Rate the full surviving pool without availability information.
4. Select the 500 highest-rated names with world and exercise diversity controls.
5. Run those 500 against the local federal trademark corpus using exact normalized identity and a one-edit rule for compact names.
6. Produce the full register plus a much smaller decision slate.

Trademark screening is preliminary only. It does not cover all phonetic or conceptual similarity, state records, common-law rights, domains, social handles, international registers, or legal likelihood of confusion.
