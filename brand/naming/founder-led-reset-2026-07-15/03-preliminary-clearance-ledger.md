# Preliminary Clearance Ledger

> **As of:** 2026-07-15  
> **Market and category:** United States launch; anti-aging skincare, cosmetics, related education/retail, and plausible wellness expansion.  
> **Purpose:** Prevent creative candidates from consuming founder attention before obvious legal or commercial conflicts are found. This is a knockout screen, not a legal opinion or substitute for a comprehensive attorney search.

## Decision key

- **RED — retire:** A direct or severe related-category conflict makes pursuit commercially imprudent.
- **YELLOW — hold for counsel:** No decisive knockout, but material uncertainty or close marks require attorney review before further creative investment.
- **PROVISIONAL PASS — presentable:** No material conflict found in the documented preliminary screen. This does not mean legally cleared.

## UPFRNT — evidence

1. [Upfront Cosmetics](https://upfrontcosmetics.ca/) is a current beauty and personal-care business with an active [skincare collection](https://upfrontcosmetics.ca/collections/skincare).
2. Its [mission](https://upfrontcosmetics.ca/pages/missiohttps-upfrontcosmetics-ca-pages-missionn) and [about page](https://upfrontcosmetics.ca/pages/about-us) center research, understandable information, customer education, and transparency—the same intended strategic territory as this brand.
3. It has advertised renewed [shipping to the United States](https://upfrontcosmetics.ca/blogs/be-upfront/usa-shipping-is-back-and-better-than-ever).
4. **UPFRONT**, U.S. serial **79327545**, registration **7082491**, is live and registered to Upfront B.V. Its Class 3 goods expressly include cosmetics, skin cleansers/body wash, body lotion/butter, exfoliating creams, sun care, vitamin facial creams/oils, and day/night creams; related classes include supplements, retail, and health information. [Official USPTO TSDR record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=79327545&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch).
5. USPTO guidance explains that marks do not need identical spellings to conflict; similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, and commercial impression matters. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion).

### UPFRNT conclusion

The marks would sound identical, look nearly identical, mean the same thing, occupy directly overlapping categories and channels, and communicate nearly the same brand idea. **Do not pursue.**

## Mandatory screen before any future name is presented

Each candidate must have a dated evidence row covering:

1. Exact spelling, spacing, plural, and obvious vowel-deletion variants.
2. Phonetic equivalents and close visual/conceptual variants.
3. Federal records in Classes 3, 5, 35, and 44, plus related coordinated classes where relevant.
4. Current U.S. common-law use: brands, products, services, retailers, marketplaces, business records, and social handles.
5. Meaningful Canadian and international skincare/cosmetics use.
6. Exact-domain status and whether search/social results are realistically ownable.
7. A written **RED**, **YELLOW**, or **PROVISIONAL PASS** decision with source links.

Only candidates rated **PROVISIONAL PASS** may be shown to the founder. Finalists then go to a U.S. trademark attorney for a comprehensive clearance search and registrability opinion before commitment.
