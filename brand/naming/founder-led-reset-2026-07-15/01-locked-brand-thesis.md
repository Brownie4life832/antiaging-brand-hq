# Locked Brand Thesis and Naming Worlds

> **Status:** Founder-selected strategic direction, 2026-07-15.  
> **Scope:** Controls the founder-led naming reset. This replaces the prior round's inferred “self-possession and intelligent care” assumption.

## Brand thesis

> **We do not ask customers to trust skincare marketing. We show our work—explaining what every formula does, how strong the evidence is, why we made each choice, and where the limits are.**

## Strategic structure

- **Belief:** Customers deserve understanding, not manufactured authority.
- **Enemy:** Opaque formulas, inflated claims, ingredient theater, proprietary mystique used as a substitute for proof, and condescending marketing.
- **Promise:** Advanced skincare whose superiority is demonstrated and explained.
- **Customer identity:** Intelligent adults who want visible results and expect to be treated as capable decision-makers.
- **Reason to believe:** Advanced skincare knowledge, disciplined formulation, evidence grading, claims transparency, and education that reveals why a product is better.
- **Primary name job:** Signal credible openness, proof, or informed skincare with an immediate conceptual hook.
- **Secondary story:** Long-term skin improvement and skin longevity.
- **Tone:** Clear, confident, intelligent, and human—not sterile, academic, preachy, self-righteous, or faux-luxurious.

## Creative world 1 — Nothing Hidden

**Core idea:** The customer can see what is in the formula, what each choice does, what the evidence supports, and what the brand does not claim.

**Productive tensions:**

- Open, but not simplistic.
- Expert, but not secretive.
- Transparent, but not morally performative.
- Clear, but still distinctive enough to own.

**Preferred naming mechanisms:**

1. A direct, category-relevant concept with a second meaning.
2. A familiar openness or visibility word altered in one legible way.
3. An authentic object, practice, or technical term whose function is to reveal what is otherwise hidden.

## Creative world 2 — Show the Work

**Core idea:** Every claim has a visible path back to formulation logic, testing, sources, and evidence.

**Productive tensions:**

- Proof, but not courtroom severity.
- Rigor, but not a cold laboratory.
- Demonstration, but not defensive argument.
- Performance, but not inflated certainty.

**Preferred naming mechanisms:**

1. A direct proof or demonstration concept that remains consumer-friendly.
2. An authentic testing, measurement, or evidence term with a simple story.
3. A compact phrase or compound that communicates earned claims.

## Creative world 3 — Better Informed

**Core idea:** The brand transfers knowledge to the customer instead of preserving an expert–consumer power gap.

**Productive tensions:**

- Educational, but never teacherly.
- Knowledgeable, but not jargon-dependent.
- Respectful, but not passive.
- Advanced, but understandable.

**Preferred naming mechanisms:**

1. A direct understanding or knowledge concept with skin relevance.
2. An authentic learning, reading, or scientific-insight term.
3. A familiar word given one transparent structural modification.

## Explicitly retired from this reset

- Abstract self-possession or attitude words such as **Restraint**, **Bearing**, **Regard**, and **Leeway**.
- Soft shelter, repair, nature, craft, and faux-Latinate luxury aesthetics from the earlier corpus.
- Names that become appealing only after an etymology lecture.
- Generic “truth” virtue words with no distinctive mechanism.
- Volume targets, decimal scoring, AI consensus, and availability-led creative selection.
