# Post-User-Test Structural Reset

> **As of:** 2026-07-15  
> **Launch market:** United States  
> **Category:** Anti-aging skincare, cosmetics, related retail and education  
> **Status:** Preliminary knockout screening only. A surviving name still requires a comprehensive attorney-led search before adoption.

## Decision

**WRITTEN ALL OVER is creatively retired.**

**Subsequent founder decision, 2026-07-15: BASED ON WHAT? is also creatively retired.** It fails the corrected category-first gate: without explanation or a skincare descriptor, the name does not tell a cold audience what the company sells. Its skeptical attitude is strategically relevant, but the prior round mistook that attitude for the category.

The 40–50-person test produced moderate approval but no meaningful excitement. That is enough evidence to stop treating it as an incumbent. It may be intelligent and strategically explainable, but it did not create the instinctive response required of the masterbrand.

This round did not optimize for another elegant transparency phrase. It searched for a name with tension: something a customer can repeat, take a position on, and understand more deeply after learning how the company operates.

## What changed

The earlier rounds repeatedly selected names that were:

- strategically accurate after explanation;
- tasteful and editorial;
- safe enough to earn moderate approval;
- insufficiently charged to create preference.

The new screen therefore required all five conditions below:

1. The name must create a reaction before its rationale is explained.
2. It must express a customer attitude, not merely describe the company's transparency.
3. The skincare context must make the name sharper rather than merely make sense.
4. It must support a durable masterbrand, not only a campaign or content series.
5. It must survive a preliminary legal, commercial, category-use, and domain/search knockout.

## Result

**Only one new candidate earned founder attention: BASED ON WHAT?**

It turns the brand's operating model into the customer's question. Every efficacy statement, ingredient choice, concentration, comparison, and recommendation has to answer it. The name respects an intelligent customer because it does not ask for belief; it invites scrutiny.

It also has the tension missing from WRITTEN ALL OVER. It can sound skeptical, demanding, funny, or incisive depending on delivery. That makes it retellable. The strategic platform is immediate:

> **BASED ON WHAT?**  
> Every claim deserves an answer.

### Preliminary clearance judgment

**PROVISIONAL PASS WITH APPAREL, DOMAIN, AND WEAK-MARK ADJACENCY.**

- The only exact U.S. federal record found was abandoned in 2007 and covered bumper stickers in Class 16, not beauty, skincare, retail, or education. [BASED ON WHAT?, SN 78681673](https://trademarks.justia.com/786/81/based-on-what-78681673.html)
- No exact skincare, cosmetics, beauty-service, or beauty-education source identity surfaced in the documented exact and category searches.
- An exact-name Shopify store has sold shirts and accessories since 2024. That is not a direct skincare conflict, but it creates a real apparel/merchandise and common-law adjacency that counsel must investigate. [Based On What? Shopify listing](https://www.merchantgenius.io/shop/url/basedonwhat.shop)
- The exact `.com` resolves and is not presently available for a clean hand registration.
- Because the wording is a common question, protection may be narrower than for a coined word. Prominent, consistent use as a house mark would matter.

This is not a legal clearance. It is the sole candidate from this round that combines a credible preliminary knockout result with enough brand tension to justify attorney time.

## Recommendation

Do not run another 40–50-person preference poll yet. First, the founder should decide whether **BASED ON WHAT?** produces a genuine reaction. If it does, the next steps are:

1. commission an expedited attorney-led comprehensive search, including state/common-law, foreign-equivalent, domain, social, and apparel-merchandise analysis;
2. build two neutral package/nameboard executions to test whether the question reads as confident scrutiny rather than hostility;
3. test unaided recall, intended meaning, trust, and negative interpretation—not simple approval;
4. compare it against a no-name control and the strongest current incumbent, rather than against a long menu of weaker names.

If **BASED ON WHAT?** is not emotionally right for the founder, reject it quickly. The next creative search should start from a new strategic territory, not create more proof, documentation, or editorial idioms.
