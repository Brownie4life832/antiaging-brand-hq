# Category-First Recovery Round

> **As of:** 2026-07-15  
> **Launch market:** United States  
> **Category:** Anti-aging skincare, cosmetics, related retail and education  
> **Status:** Preliminary knockout screening only. Every survivor still requires a comprehensive attorney-led search before adoption.

## Founder correction

> “That name doesn't work at all. We've strayed too far from skincare.”

The correction is right. The prior rounds made the brand's philosophy—proof, transparency, documentation, skepticism—the naming subject and expected packaging or explanation to supply the category. That reversed the required hierarchy.

The new hierarchy is:

1. **The name must register as skin, face, or skincare within roughly two seconds.**
2. **It must then reveal the brand's proof, education, or transparency idea.**
3. **It must still have enough tension to work as a masterbrand rather than a clinic, content series, or campaign.**

If the first condition fails, the candidate does not reach the founder regardless of how elegant its strategic explanation is.

## Why the process kept drifting

- **Ethos became subject matter.** Transparency is how the company should behave; it does not automatically tell a cold audience what the company sells.
- **Category relevance was scored instead of gated.** A high score for cleverness or strategy could compensate for a low skincare score. It no longer can.
- **We searched outward from favored language mechanisms.** WORKS CITED, NO COVER STORY, and WRITTEN ALL OVER led to more documents, stories, and proof idioms even after that territory stopped producing category-ownable names.
- **We evaluated with the strategy visible.** The founder and naming team knew every rationale. A customer encountering one word on a screen or shelf does not.
- **Explainability masqueraded as strength.** A name that makes sense after a paragraph can still be weak before the paragraph. The 40–50-person WRITTEN ALL OVER result demonstrated that distinction.

## Revised presentation gates

A candidate is shown only if it passes all six:

1. **Cold category test:** skin, face, skincare, or an unmistakable category term is the likely first association.
2. **Intrinsic hook test:** the second meaning is already in the words; a strategy deck does not create it.
3. **Masterbrand test:** it can plausibly sit on a moisturizer, serum, and sunscreen without reading only as an article, slogan, or clinic.
4. **Founder-taste test:** it has the relevance of TIMELINE or BAREFACED, the directness of DOLLAR SHAVE CLUB, or the immediate tension of LIQUID DEATH.
5. **Adversarial test:** the strongest negative reading is survivable and does not contradict advanced knowledge or customer respect.
6. **Clearance gate:** live federal exact and near-mark queries, current common-law/category use, obvious international use, and exact-domain status are documented before presentation.

## Provisional finalists

### SKIN, UNREDACTED

**Creative case:** SKIN supplies the category immediately. UNREDACTED makes the transparency promise active and visual: no hidden rationale, missing concentrations, buried tradeoffs, or selective evidence. It has more tension than a reassuring adjective-plus-SKIN construction and can support a distinctive disclosure system across packaging and education.

**Adversarial case:** UNREDACTED carries government, legal-document, and scandal associations. It is four syllables and may still lean editorial. The brand would need to earn the promise operationally; selective disclosure would be especially damaging under this name.

**Preliminary screen:** Live USPTO searches returned no exact SKIN UNREDACTED, UNREDACTED SKIN, or UNREDACTED-family federal record. Exact beauty/skincare and company searches surfaced no source-identifying use. `skinunredacted.com` and `unredactedskin.com` returned no Verisign `.com` registration record when checked. **Provisional pass, subject to the final hostile audit and attorney clearance.**

### SKIN, UNABRIDGED

**Creative case:** SKIN is immediate; UNABRIDGED means complete rather than shortened. It captures the full story of formula, mechanism, evidence, limitations, and skin education. It is the cleanest masterbrand shape in the round.

**Adversarial case:** It is literary and slightly rarefied. Search results include unrelated audiobook/book formatting such as “Skin (Unabridged),” creating media-title noise. It may earn intellectual admiration without emotional desire—the same danger exposed by WRITTEN ALL OVER.

**Preliminary screen:** Live USPTO exact, joined, and relevant UNABRIDGED-family searches found no beauty/skincare conflict. Exact common-law beauty searches found no source-identifying use. `skinunabridged.com` returned no Verisign `.com` registration record when checked. **Provisional pass, with media-title and weak-mark adjacency; attorney clearance required.**

### SKINCARE FOR SKEPTICS

**Creative case:** This is the clearest correction to the prior drift. The category is impossible to miss, the customer has an identity, and the evidence-first operating model is embedded in the proposition. It has the explicitness of DOLLAR SHAVE CLUB and enough friction to generate a real reaction.

**Adversarial case:** It can read as a book, newsletter, campaign, or descriptive audience statement rather than a protectable house mark. “Skeptics” can make the brand sound permanently defensive, and the wording may receive narrow protection even if registrable.

**Preliminary screen:** Live USPTO searches returned no exact SKINCARE FOR SKEPTICS or SKIN CARE FOR SKEPTICS record and no live SKEPTIC/SKEPTICS Class 3 record. Exact common-law searches found ordinary descriptive and hashtag use but no exact source-identifying skincare brand. `skincareforskeptics.com` returned no Verisign `.com` registration record when checked. **Provisional pass with descriptiveness/failure-to-function risk; attorney clearance required.**

## Current recommendation

Do not return to an abstract proof name. Compare the strongest provocative proposition, **SKINCARE FOR SKEPTICS**, against the strongest masterbrand construction, **SKIN, UNREDACTED**. Keep **SKIN, UNABRIDGED** only as the more elegant control.

The next evaluation should not ask “Which do you like?” It should ask, cold and without explanation:

1. What does this company sell?
2. What kind of company do you expect it to be?
3. What is the name promising?
4. What, if anything, feels negative or untrustworthy?
5. Which name can you recall ten minutes later without seeing the list?

A final adoption decision still requires comprehensive counsel-led clearance. USPTO itself advises that exact-word knockout searching is only the first step and that alternative spellings, pronunciations, related wording, and other sources must also be reviewed. [USPTO federal-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)
