# Clearance-First Naming Round: Skin Layers as One Exploratory Lane

> **As of:** 2026-07-15  
> **Launch market:** United States  
> **Category:** Anti-aging skincare, cosmetics, related retail and education, with plausible wellness expansion  
> **Status:** Preliminary knockout screening only. This is not a legal opinion or a substitute for comprehensive attorney-led clearance.

## What changed in this round

- More than 100 candidates were generated privately across three lanes: skin layers/anatomy, proof and transparency, and founder-preferred naming constructions.
- Skin anatomy was intentionally limited to one lane. It was not allowed to dominate the round.
- Candidates were screened before creative presentation for exact and close federal marks, direct-category common-law use, meaningful international use, and basic domain/search ownability.
- A second review removed names that looked technically open but were commercially derivative, weak, or too crowded to justify founder attention.

## Names that survived both gates

### 1. NO COVER STORY

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS - strongest overall candidate in this round.

**Intrinsic idea:** No invented marketing story and no need to cover the face. The name joins transparency with bare-face confidence in one familiar phrase.

**Knockout findings:**

- Exact, joined, spaced, and obvious close-variant searches did not surface a relevant live U.S. federal mark in Classes 3, 5, 35, or 44.
- No active exact-name skincare, cosmetics, beauty, or wellness brand surfaced in targeted common-law and marketplace searches.
- The phrase has editorial use in no-makeup and skincare writing, but no source-brand use was identified in the screen.
- `nocoverstory.com` did not resolve to an active website at the time checked. That is not proof that the registration is available, and registrar plus historical-use checks remain necessary.

**Primary liability:** Three words and an editorial flavor. It could be read as anti-makeup unless the brand story keeps the focus on honest evidence and skin good enough not to conceal.

**Why it advances:** It is the only candidate in the round that combines two founder-preferred territories - nothing hidden and bare-face confidence - without requiring an etymology lesson.

### 2. WORKS CITED

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH ADJACENCY - advance only if the name itself creates genuine excitement.

**Intrinsic idea:** Every meaningful claim shows its sources. The name can turn citations, evidence grading, and formula notes into a coherent brand system.

**Knockout findings:**

- Exact, joined, spaced, and close-variant U.S. federal searches did not surface a relevant live mark in Classes 3, 5, 35, or 44.
- No active exact-name skincare or cosmetics brand surfaced in targeted common-law, marketplace, or international searches.
- Exact common-law use exists as the title of a culture/fashion blog operated by clothing brand Sam Brewbaker: https://sambrewbaker.com/blogs/news/about
- The exact `.com` is registered and redirects elsewhere. `workscitedskin.com`, `workscitedskincare.com`, and `workscitedbeauty.com` appeared unregistered on 2026-07-15, subject to immediate registrar confirmation.
- The phrase is a standard academic heading, so generic search competition will remain substantial: https://style.mla.org/bibliography-references/

**Primary liability:** It can feel academic or editorial, and counsel must assess the existing fashion/lifestyle use, especially if the brand intends to build education, publishing, or apparel extensions.

**Why it advances:** It is the most literal and ownable expression found for the brand's `show the work` behavior, even though it is not directly skin-coded.

### 3. SURFACE & STRUCTURE

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH WEAK-MARK RISK - sole layer-adjacent survivor.

**Intrinsic idea:** Serious skincare should understand what appears at the surface and what supports it structurally. It gives the layer exploration a consumer-readable expression without using crowded anatomy words.

**Knockout findings:**

- Exact and close-variant federal searches did not surface a relevant live mark in the target classes.
- No active exact-name skincare or cosmetics brand surfaced in targeted common-law and marketplace searches.
- The words are used descriptively in skincare and skin-longevity discussions, including Yin Your Skin's three-level philosophy: https://yinyourskin.com/pages/skincare

**Primary liability:** Both words are descriptive and carry strong architecture/design associations. Protectability may be narrow, and any suggestion that a topical product changes deep tissue structure must be kept out of unsupported claims.

**Why it advances:** It is the only layer concept that survived without feeling like a dermatology clinic, a product technology, or a forced scientific coinage. It remains an exploratory candidate, not the current recommendation.

### 4. KNOWN QUANTITY

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS - strategically sound, creatively secondary.

**Intrinsic idea:** Customers know what is in the formula, how much is there, what it is expected to do, and where the evidence stops.

**Knockout findings:**

- Exact and close-variant federal searches did not surface a relevant live mark in the target classes.
- No active exact-name skincare, cosmetics, or wellness identity surfaced in targeted common-law and marketplace searches.
- `knownquantity.com` resolves to parking infrastructure rather than a clean acquisition path.
- The name relies on the ordinary idiom meaning a person or thing whose qualities are already understood: https://www.dictionary.com/browse/known-quantity

**Primary liability:** It is impersonal, mathematical, and not skin-coded. It is included because it passed both screens, not because it currently beats NO COVER STORY or WORKS CITED.

## Strong-looking candidates deliberately rejected

These are recorded to prevent accidental recycling; they are not recommendations.

- **TIMECOURSE:** No exact direct-category mark surfaced, but the name is too close in concept and market position to science-led longevity/skincare brand TIMELINE. TIMELINE has live U.S. registration 6,504,913 covering medicated cosmetics and skincare. Official record: https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88570397&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch
- **SKIN IN FULL:** Too close to live SKINFUL BEAUTY skincare registrations, including serials 88976600 and 88178063.
- **LAYERWISE:** Active exact-name skincare platform: https://www.getlayerwise.com/
- **SKIN ATLAS:** Active exact-name New York skin studio: https://www.skinatlas.nyc/about
- **EVERY LAYER:** Current skincare slogan territory, including Anokha's `for every layer of you`: https://www.anokhaskincare.com/pages/about
- **SKIN MECHANICS:** No live exact mark found, but the dead singular SKIN MECHANIC application and generic scientific use make the term weak, cold, and service-like. Official dead record: https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=76162873&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch
- **RECEIPTS:** Live exact U.S. registration 6,534,833 covers cosmetics and broad skincare: https://trademarks.justia.com/903/01/receipts-90301921.html
- **SKINSPAN:** Pending exact skincare/cosmetics application 79432080: https://trademarks.justia.com/794/32/skinspan-79432080.html
- **LONG GAME:** Fresh exact filing 99901257 covers cosmetics and facial serums: https://uspto.report/TM/99901257
- **FULL STORY:** Active exact U.S. registration 3,194,193 covers cosmetics: https://trademarks.justia.com/788/71/full-78871114.html

## Current judgment

1. **NO COVER STORY** is the only name from this round I would currently put into a final-decision test.
2. **WORKS CITED** is the credible challenger if a more evidence-forward, editorial system is attractive.
3. **SURFACE & STRUCTURE** is the answer to the layer exploration, but its weak-mark and architecture liabilities keep it behind the first two.
4. **KNOWN QUANTITY** is a useful strategic control, not a recommended winner.

Before filing, packaging, domain acquisition, or material launch spend, any finalist still requires a comprehensive search and registrability opinion from U.S. trademark counsel.
