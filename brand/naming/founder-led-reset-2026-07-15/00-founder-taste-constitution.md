# Founder Taste Constitution

> **Status:** Founder-led reset, working constitution based on direct responses on 2026-07-15.  
> **Purpose:** Replace inferred AI taste with explicit creative preferences. This document controls the next naming round.

The founder could not identify successful brand names they actively hate. Do not manufacture an anti-reference set or infer broad bans without repeated evidence.

## Preferred naming architectures

Founder ranking:

1. **Direct, category-relevant concept** — model: Timeline, Barefaced.
2. **Familiar word subtly modified** — model: Comfrt.
3. **Authentic technical or insider term** — model: Peloton.
4. **Explicit benefit or proposition** — model: Native Deodorant, Dollar Shave Club.
5. **Bold contradiction or provocation** — model: Liquid Death.

## What the ranking means

- The name should contain an intrinsic idea a customer can grasp without a strategy presentation.
- Relevance and conceptual clarity outrank semantic neutrality.
- A coined name is welcome when the source word remains obvious; arbitrary luxury-sounding coinages are not supported by current evidence.
- Some category specificity is acceptable. Do not sacrifice a sharp idea merely to preserve hypothetical portfolio headroom.
- Technical terminology is useful only when authentic, pronounceable, and capable of expanding into a consumer story.
- Provocation is an available accent, not the lead mechanism.

## Primary subject hierarchy

Founder ranking:

1. **Evidence, honesty, and no hype.**
2. **Time and skin longevity.**
3. **Visible anti-aging performance.**
4. **Confidence in the natural or bare face.**
5. **Barrier strength and healthier skin.**
6. **Personalized or custom formulation.**

## Naming-world hierarchy

Founder ranking:

1. **Nothing Hidden** — openness, disclosure, visibility, formula transparency.
2. **Show the Work** — proof, testing, sources, evidence, demonstration.
3. **Better Informed** — knowledge, understanding, education, expert guidance.

## Founder-authored strategic input

> “The main focus of the brand ethos will be incredible transparency and education while respecting the customer. We're talking about our advanced knowledge of skincare and proving why the product is better.”

## Working decision sentence

We will choose the name that creates an immediate, relevant idea about openness, proof, or informed skincare, even if that makes the brand less neutral or requires one simple verbal hook.

## Controls for the next round

- Maximum three coherent creative worlds.
- Maximum six presented names per world in the first response round.
- No AI consensus voting or simulated buyer panels.
- Founder reaction determines the second-round brief.
- A rationale may reveal an intrinsic hook; it may not rescue a dead or misleading word.
- **Clearance is a presentation gate.** No candidate reaches the founder until it has passed a documented preliminary knockout screen covering exact and phonetically similar marks, closely related goods/services, common-law commercial use, meaningful international conflicts, and basic domain/search ownability.
- A preliminary pass is not a legal opinion. The final two or three still require comprehensive attorney-led clearance before filing, packaging, or material launch spend.
