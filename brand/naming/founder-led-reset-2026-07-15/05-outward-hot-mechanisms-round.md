# Outward Round from the Two Hot Mechanisms

> **As of:** 2026-07-15  
> **Launch market:** United States  
> **Category:** Anti-aging skincare, cosmetics, related retail and education  
> **Status:** Preliminary knockout screening only. None of these names is legally cleared; a finalist still requires a comprehensive attorney search.

## What the founder reaction tells us

The promising territory is not skin anatomy. It is a familiar phrase that acquires a sharp second meaning when placed on a skincare bottle:

- **NO COVER STORY** turns ordinary editorial language into anti-concealment and anti-spin.
- **WORKS CITED** turns an academic convention into a product-evidence behavior.
- **KNOWN QUANTITY** partly works because it turns a familiar idiom into formula precision.

The outward round therefore explored three mechanisms: accountable promises, evidence that travels with the product, and ordinary language that becomes facial or quantitative in context.

## New names worth founder attention

### 1. WRITTEN ALL OVER

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH WEAK-MARK RISK.

**Why it works:** It completes itself as “written all over your face.” The results can be visible on the face while the formula, evidence, and limitations are fully documented. It is the strongest new double meaning in the round.

**Knockout findings:** No exact live U.S. target-category filing surfaced in the indexed federal sweep. The closest indexed wording was a longer pet-food slogan, **OUR FOOD HAS YOUR PET'S NAME WRITTEN ALL OVER IT**, registration 4,731,643, which was cancelled in 2025. No exact skincare, cosmetics, haircare, perfume, or beauty source surfaced in U.S. or targeted English-language international searches. Exact beauty results use the phrase descriptively rather than as a brand. `writtenallover.com` had no A record when checked; that is not proof of registration availability.

**Primary liability:** The phrase is common and may receive narrower protection. Without disciplined copy, it could also suggest that aging or stress is “written all over” the customer's face.

### 2. HOLD US TO IT

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS.

**Why it works:** Transparency becomes accountability. The brand does not ask for trust; it invites customers to inspect concentrations, sources, claims, and results.

**Knockout findings:** No exact or close live U.S. target-category mark surfaced, including obvious **HOLD ME TO IT** variants. No exact skincare, cosmetics, supplement, or beauty-service source surfaced. International exact-use results were ordinary accountability language, not a beauty identity. `holdustoit.com`, `.co`, and the skin-specific fallback checked did not return active DNS records.

**Primary liability:** It may initially sound like a campaign line rather than a house name, and every claim becomes a promise the customer can enforce.

### 3. KEEPS ITS WORD

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH DESCRIPTIVE-USE ADJACENCY.

**Why it works:** It gives the formula a human standard: say exactly what it will do, then do it. It is warmer and less combative than HOLD US TO IT.

**Knockout findings:** No exact or close live U.S. target-category mark surfaced. No exact skincare identity surfaced. AspireLIFE and Plantifique use “keeps its word” descriptively in skincare copy, which supports comprehension but slightly weakens exclusivity. No exact international cosmetics identity surfaced. `keepsitsword.com` and the checked skin/skincare/beauty fallbacks returned no Verisign RDAP record on the check date.

**Primary liability:** The category is not obvious, the grammar is unusual for a house mark, and it creates a real claim-substantiation burden.

### 4. COMPARE NOTES

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH DOMAIN AND WEAK-MARK RISK.

**Why it works:** Education becomes a conversation rather than a lecture. The brand can compare formulas, evidence, routines, and customer observations while respecting the customer's intelligence.

**Knockout findings:** No exact live U.S. target-category filing or exact beauty source surfaced in the indexed sweep. Beauty and skincare publishers use the idiom descriptively, not as a source identifier. No meaningful exact international cosmetics identity surfaced. `comparenotes.com` is registered and listed for sale for $12,299, so the clean exact domain is available only as a premium acquisition at the time checked.

**Primary liability:** It is softer and less authoritative than WORKS CITED, generic search competition is substantial, and trademark scope may be narrow.

### 5. THIS MUCH

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH DOMAIN AND WEAK-MARK RISK.

**Why it works:** It turns exact concentrations into a natural packaging grammar: **THIS MUCH / 0.1% RETINAL**. It is human and gestural rather than mathematical, with a subtle affection undertone.

**Knockout findings:** No exact live U.S. target-category mark or exact skincare identity surfaced. Close marks including **I LOVE YOU THIS MUCH**, **LOVE YOU THIS MUCH**, and **EAT THIS MUCH** are in unrelated classes. The exact `.com` is registered; checked skin/skincare/beauty fallbacks did not return a Verisign RDAP record.

**Primary liability:** Category-opaque, generic, search-noisy, and probably narrower to protect.

### 6. SOURCES INCLUDED

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS WITH DESCRIPTIVENESS RISK.

**Why it works:** A “batteries included” construction for evidence: the proof travels with the product rather than living in a vague “science-backed” claim.

**Knockout findings:** No exact live U.S. target-category mark, direct beauty use, or meaningful exact international cosmetics identity surfaced in the indexed sweep. Results were ordinary academic and analytics prose. `sourcesincluded.com` had no A record when checked; that is not proof of registration availability.

**Primary liability:** It may be perceived as a slogan rather than a house name and could be difficult to monopolize.

### 7. NO TALL TALES

**Decision:** PROVISIONAL PASS.

**Why it works:** It rejects miracle stories and exaggerated skincare mythology in a familiar phrase. It preserves some of NO COVER STORY's provocation without repeating its exact device.

**Knockout findings:** No exact live U.S. target-category filing surfaced. The closest live federal result was **TALL TALES** for comedy, registration 5,983,221; an older wine registration is cancelled. No exact skincare, cosmetics, supplement, or beauty-service source surfaced. Exact non-category uses include a trading card and a UK finance campaign line. `notalltales.com` and checked skin/skincare fallbacks had no active DNS records.

**Primary liability:** It can feel folksy, juvenile, or Western, and it is directionally similar to the stronger NO COVER STORY.

## Screened but withheld from founder presentation

- **AS TESTED:** Technically promising, but it is colder, can evoke animal testing, and implies finished-formula testing that the operating model must substantiate.
- **FORMULA FOOTNOTES:** Strong system idea, but live **FOOTNOTE** publishing rights create pressure for the planned education/content arm; it is also too derivative of WORKS CITED for this outward round.
- **IN FULL VIEW:** Withheld after exact common-law use and `infullview.com` history surfaced in the HR/video-interviewing category.
- **MATERIAL WITNESS:** Withheld because the legal/criminal tone overwhelms the ingredient-and-evidence wordplay.
- **LET THE RECORD SHOW:** Withheld because the exact phrase is already a notable book title, podcast title, and active domain/search territory.
- **ALL ACCOUNTED FOR:** Withheld because exact accounting businesses and the exact domain are active.
- **SEE ATTACHED:** No direct beauty conflict surfaced, but the exact domain is active and the phrase reads more like office copy than a durable skincare house.

## Current creative ranking

1. **WRITTEN ALL OVER** — best new double meaning.
2. **HOLD US TO IT** — strongest statement of customer respect and accountability.
3. **KEEPS ITS WORD** — warmest accountability name.
4. **COMPARE NOTES** — best conversational education name.
5. **THIS MUCH** — best packaging-system idea.
6. **SOURCES INCLUDED** — clear proposition, weaker as a house mark.
7. **NO TALL TALES** — memorable, but likely inferior to NO COVER STORY.

The two hot incumbents remain **NO COVER STORY** and **WORKS CITED**. The purpose of this round is to find names capable of displacing them, not to expand an undifferentiated list.
