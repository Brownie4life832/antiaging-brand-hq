# First Authored Naming Worlds

> **Clearance update, 2026-07-15:** **UPFRNT** and **Skin Fluent / SKINFLUENT** are retired after preliminary knockout searches found direct skincare conflicts. The remaining names in this historical creative document have not been cleared and must not be presented as viable candidates without first passing the new clearance gate.

> **Status:** Historical founder-calibrated creative response, superseded by the clearance-first rule on 2026-07-15.  
> **Important:** These are creative records, not availability claims or trademark recommendations. No uncleared name in this document may be presented again as a viable candidate.

## Presentation rule

Present the names naked first. Capture **hot / warm / cold** within five seconds. Reveal the intrinsic hook only after the reaction is recorded. Do not average, score, or ask simulated judges to vote.

## World 1 — Nothing Hidden

### Naked names

1. **Frontlabel**
2. **UPFRNT**
3. **Open Label**
4. **Formula in Full**
5. **To Be Clear**
6. **Cross Section**

### Intrinsic hooks — reveal after reaction

| Name | Intrinsic hook | Primary creative liability |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontlabel** | Moves concentrations, evidence grades, limitations, and sourcing from the back label to the front; packaging becomes the operating proof. | May initially sound like a labeling company if skincare context is weak. |
| **UPFRNT** | *Upfront* with one vowel removed: direct candor plus the Comfrt-style construction the founder admires. | Compressed spelling may feel like a technology brand or trend device. |
| **Open Label** | Literal formula-label transparency plus an authentic research term: in an open-label study, treatment information is not concealed. | Can sound like packaging infrastructure or scientific publishing. |
| **Formula in Full** | The entire formulation logic and evidence story, not a hero-ingredient headline. | Longer and more proposition-like than a conventional house mark. |
| **To Be Clear** | A natural preface to an honest explanation; “clear” also belongs to skincare language. | Could imply acne/clarifying products or sound slightly defensive. |
| **Cross Section** | A scientific view through skin layers that reveals internal structure; a Peloton-style authentic term with a visual system built in. | Has engineering, architecture, and anatomy associations outside skincare. |

## World 2 — Show the Work

### Naked names

1. **Test of Time**
2. **Better Because**
3. **Proof Included**
4. **Lab to Label**
5. **Timepoint**
6. **EVIDNCE**

### Intrinsic hooks — reveal after reaction

| Name | Intrinsic hook | Primary creative liability |
|---|---|---|
| **Test of Time** | Combines testing and skin longevity in one familiar phrase; the formula faces the test, not the customer fighting age. | Can imply heritage or nostalgia unless the identity feels advanced. |
| **Better Because** | The brand says “better” only when it can finish the sentence with formulation and evidence. | Deliberately incomplete syntax; needs confident execution. |
| **Proof Included** | Evidence is part of the product experience through a proof card, QR dossier, sources, and limitations. | Broad outside skincare and creates a high substantiation burden. |
| **Lab to Label** | Makes the complete path from research and formulation to the claims on the package visible. | Could resemble a formulation consultancy without strong consumer design. |
| **Timepoint** | The clinical-research term for a scheduled moment when results are measured, joined to the brand's longevity story. | Technical and initially category-ambiguous. |
| **EVIDNCE** | *Evidence* with one unstressed vowel removed—the Comfrt mechanism applied to the defining brand idea. | Colder than the phrase names and may need spelling reinforcement. |

## World 3 — Better Informed

### Naked names

1. **Skin, Explained**
2. **Skin Fluent**
3. **Skinformed**
4. **Know Why**
5. **Why It Works**
6. **Formula Notes**

### Intrinsic hooks — reveal after reaction

| Name | Intrinsic hook | Primary creative liability |
|---|---|---|
| **Skin, Explained** | The category and mission resolve immediately; it creates a repeatable “Retinoids, explained” and “Your formula, explained” system. | Descriptive and may read as an educational platform before a product brand. |
| **Skin Fluent** | Expert command of skincare plus the customer's ability to become fluent in their own skin. | Could resemble a service, curriculum, or content channel. |
| **Skinformed** | An immediately readable fusion of *skin* and *informed*, combining the Barefaced and Comfrt preference signals. | May feel ingenious or overly pun-driven; the first reaction decides. |
| **Know Why** | The customer receives reasons instead of instructions or demands for trust. | Broad and potentially awkward beside some product descriptors. |
| **Why It Works** | Puts efficacy and explanation into the name as an explicit commercial proposition. | Long and more like a content franchise than a conventional mark. |
| **Formula Notes** | Publishes the thinking, evidence, trade-offs, and decisions behind every formula. | Editorial and potentially quieter than the founder's performance ambition. |

## Corpus control

No exact match for these 18 names was found in the local prior naming corpus. That is not an availability finding.
