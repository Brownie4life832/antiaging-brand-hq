## Generation and review lenses applied

The names visibly apply these generation lenses: founder-name-DNA reverse engineering; competitor and category-code inversion; anti-descriptor; benefit ladder; semantic escape; semiotic tension; conceptual blending; compound collision; controlled clipping and mutation; embedded `skin`/`face` wordplay; authentic insider terminology; customer-credo fragments; explicit propositions; bold contradiction; and negative-space benefits. Review was limited to corpus deduplication, naked-name judgment, basic pronunciation/spelling, product syntax, obvious harmful associations, claim caution, and portfolio headroom.

Still pending on any shortlist: respondent-based five-second category testing, hear-to-spell and delayed-recall measurement, premium/warmth/effectiveness semantic differentials, native-speaker linguistic review, and live confusing-similarity/common-law/trademark clearance.

## Screening note

The 250 above passed creative deduplication, basic pronunciation, obvious harmful-association, product-syntax, and brief-fit review. They have **not** passed live trademark or common-law clearance. Under USPTO guidance, a real clearance search must test similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, and overall commercial impression for related goods—not only exact matches. The strongest candidates should next go through a documented knockout across Class 3 and relevant adjacent classes, internet/common-law use, WIPO/regional sources, domains, handles, and native-speaker linguistic review before counsel sees the final 3–5.
