- Final 300 also had zero normalized exact overlap with the concurrently developed wave 4 pool at the last audit

Normalization decomposes Unicode, removes diacritics, lowercases, and removes all non-alphanumeric characters.

## Freshness controls

The first raw divergence map deliberately sampled editorial, optics, proof-adjacent, civic, game, and quality-language territories. Those ideas were then treated mostly as controls because they repeated structures visible in waves 1 and 2. The survivor pool was rebuilt around the final decision brief's underused territories: continuous identity, personal authority, adaptive range, mature presence, no-drama competence, application micro-gestures, and authentic terminology from manufacturing, human factors, aviation, automotive systems, decision science, and performance.

Near-sibling matrices were reduced during an adversarial pass. Legal/IP-coded phrases, identity-verification language, therapy clichés, mediocrity-coded terms, generic product instructions, and especially cold B2B forms were replaced before handoff.
