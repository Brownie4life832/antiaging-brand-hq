# 250 additional net-new anti-aging brand names - wave 3

> **Scope:** These 250 were selected from 300 audited survivors and checked by normalized exact matching against a conservative 2,604-name retained baseline (2,104 traceable historical candidates plus existing waves 1-2) and a broader 3,411-name parser safety set. An independent adversarial pass removed the 50 weakest or highest-risk survivors.
>
> **Score meaning:** Creative-fit score for this specific brand before trademark, common-law, domain, social, linguistic, or regulatory clearance. Every score is strictly above 8.0/10. A high score is not an availability claim.

## Creative winners before the knockout search

These are the 12 directions I would react to first as names. The later 1,000-name knockout register - not this creative ranking - controls obvious conflict eliminations.

1. **One of One**
2. **Portrait Mode**
3. **Human Scale**
4. **Deciding Factor**
5. **Choice Architecture**
6. **Present Tense**
7. **Daily Driver**
8. **Course Made Good**
9. **Dynamic Range**
10. **Job Well Done**
11. **First Article**
12. **Readymode**

## What changed in this wave

Wave 3 moved away from proof/editorial repetition into recognizable self, customer sovereignty, adaptive capacity, mature aliveness, competent care, micro-gesture, and authentic technical-adjacent language. The final 250 preserve both human phrases and compact names, but cut legalistic, unstable, overly generic, and instruction-like constructions.
