# Founder-led naming round — locked charter

> **Status:** Locked for the next naming round on 2026-07-18.  
> **Decision owner:** Founder.  
> **Prior corpus:** Research only. Previous names may reappear naturally, but previous scores, ranks, rationales, and shortlist status carry no weight.

## Founder-defined name job

Build a name for an **exceptional, science-backed, transparent skincare brand that respects women**.

The customer should feel both:

1. **Confident in herself as she is.** The brand does not manufacture inadequacy, shame aging, or imply that she needs to become another person.
2. **Motivated to improve the health of her skin.** Confidence is not complacency; effective care and visible improvement remain desirable.

## The strategic center

> **Serious science, made transparent, in service of women who want healthier skin without being told they are inadequate.**

### Productive tension

- Confidence **and** progress.
- Scientific authority **and** human respect.
- Transparency **and** excitement.
- Visible improvement **without** age panic.
- Premium aspiration **without** condescension or mystique.

## The name's primary job

Create an immediate, exciting brand idea that makes a customer expect a modern, high-performance skincare company with unusually strong science and nothing to hide.

The name does not need to explain the entire operating model. It does need enough intrinsic energy and relevance that the skincare story feels natural rather than retrofitted.

## Founder taste controls

### Supported forms

The current positive reference set favors:

1. A direct, category-relevant concept with an immediate story.
2. A familiar word changed in one legible, purposeful way.
3. An authentic technical or insider term with a natural consumer story.
4. A bold compound, contradiction, or proposition with real voltage.

### Rejection controls

- Reject two generic words placed together unless their combination creates a surprising third meaning.
- Reject inert statements, slogans, or phrases that merely announce a virtue.
- Reject names whose skincare relevance exists only in the rationale.
- Reject names that sound like an education platform, evidence portal, agency, publication, or campaign instead of the masterbrand.
- Reject faux-luxury coinages with interchangeable siblings.
- Reject explanations that make the strategy sound stronger than the naked name.

**Founder example of a fatal failure:** `Show the Work` is an awful masterbrand name because it has no intrinsic excitement or clear skincare meaning.

## Success test

When a woman encounters the name cold, the intended response is not necessarily a complete understanding of the company. It is:

> “That sounds like a serious, modern skincare brand with a point of view. I want to know what makes it different.”

The name must then support, without strain:

- evidence-led product claims;
- full formula and decision transparency;
- respectful communication with women;
- healthier skin and visible improvement;
- a multi-product premium skincare range.

## Round architecture

### Stage 0 — founder taste diagnostic

Review representative forms from the existing corpus without scores, rationales, availability, or prior status. Identify what has energy, what feels dead, and why.

### Cycle 1 — discovery

Generate through three genuinely different approaches:

1. **Immediate conceptual relevance** — category-adjacent ideas, double meanings, and concepts with a natural skincare story.
2. **Transparent invention** — recognizable source words altered, fused, shortened, or redirected in one legible move.
3. **Authentic sideways terminology** — real terms from fields involving adaptation, calibration, maintenance, surface quality, health, identity, or improvement.

The seventeen accumulated generation moves may support these approaches, but they are not quotas and do not organize the round.

### Founder gate

No more than six names per approach reach the founder. Reactions determine which territories, forms, sounds, and levels of relevance survive.

### Cycle 2 — response

Generate only inside the surviving directions. Do not rerun the complete method or add volume without a changed hypothesis.

### Final implementation

Use naked-name evaluation, fatal usability gates, preliminary collision screening, identical prototypes, spoken use, delayed recall, and target-buyer inference. No candidate receives a better rationale, design, or presentation than another.

## Repeat policy

- Generation is blind to the old corpus.
- Historical matches are permitted.
- Deduplicate only after the new pool exists.
- Record independent resurfacing as a signal, not a vote.
- A repeated name receives neither a penalty nor a legacy advantage.

## Stop rule

“None” is permitted at the Cycle 1 founder gate if the rejection pattern is recorded and the next cycle changes the hypothesis.

If Cycle 2 also produces no credible finalist, stop AI-led volume generation. Revisit the strategic premise or bring in an independent specialist rather than starting another undiagnosed batch.
