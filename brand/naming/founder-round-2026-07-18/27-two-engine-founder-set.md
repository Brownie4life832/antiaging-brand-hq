# Two-engine founder set

Date: 2026-07-19

## Process

The age/time construction route is retired as a naming engine after the founder rejected Agerithm and identified the avenue itself as a struggle. Age confidence remains part of the brand worldview, not a requirement of the name.

This round advances only the two productive engines:

1. Candid double meanings with an immediate skincare, formulation, or evidence connection.
2. Controlled constructions built from recognizable science or formulation language.

## Why the set is intentionally small

Only three new names survived the meaning, brand-energy, beauty-conflict, and tone gates. A symmetrical set would require adding weaker science constructions, which would recreate the volume-without-quality problem.

## Preliminary knockout screen

This is not legal clearance or trademark advice. The local U.S. federal corpus was current through 2026-07-06, supplemented by a preliminary online exact-use search.

- In Plain Skin: no exact or one-edit federal record found; no exact active beauty brand found in the preliminary online screen.
- Compound Interest: live exact federal record outside the beauty classes and substantial finance/chemistry-education usage; no exact active beauty brand found in the preliminary online screen.
- Labistry: no exact federal record; one-edit live record outside the beauty classes; no exact active beauty brand found in the preliminary online screen.
- Formistry: no exact live federal record or exact active beauty brand found; an unrelated job-application tool uses the name online.

## Removed before founder review

- High Maintenance: live exact records in relevant beauty/wellness classes.
- Biomance: near BIODANCE in class 003 and carries a distracting resemblance to “bromance.”
- Formelier: near FORMULIER in class 003 and exact use elsewhere.
- Chemistria: existing chemistry-company use and a less immediate construction.
- Biovidence: interpretable but pharmaceutical in tone and less naturally pronounceable.
- Active Duty: military/male-coded tone works against the brand’s emotional job.
- Body of Evidence: strong concept, but reads more like a program or title than a premium beauty brand.
- Duty of Care, Primary Source, Human Condition, Compounder: insufficient excitement or insufficiently skincare-specific.
