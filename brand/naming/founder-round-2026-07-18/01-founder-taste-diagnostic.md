# Founder taste diagnostic

> **Purpose:** Diagnose naming form, energy, and relevance before generating anything new.  
> **Important:** These are representative names from the historical corpus, not a shortlist or recommendation. Prior rankings, rationales, and screening results are intentionally hidden.

## How to react

Read the names quickly and without researching them. Then report:

- any that have **interesting energy or a useful form**, even if you would not choose the exact name;
- the names you **strongly dislike**;
- what makes the promising names feel alive and the rejected names feel dead;
- any name that comes closest to the kind of brand object you want, even if it is still wrong.

Do not try to be consistent or diplomatic. Contradictions are useful evidence.
