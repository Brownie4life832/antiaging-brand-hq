## Interpretation

The screen is intentionally conservative and cannot determine registrability by itself. A red or amber result is a research flag, not a legal conclusion; a provisional pass is not clearance. Any founder-approved candidate must receive attorney-led U.S. clearance, broader common-law research, domain and social-handle review, and an international strategy appropriate to launch markets before adoption.
