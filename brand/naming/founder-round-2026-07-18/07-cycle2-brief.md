# Cycle 2 brief

> **Status:** Active  
> **Single mechanism:** Emotionally aspirational skincare double meaning.

## Creative target

Create a familiar, ownable expression that feels alive before explanation and rewards a second look afterward. One meaning should connect naturally to face, skin, appearance, formulation, or skin health. The other should express confidence, vitality, momentum, self-possession, or optimism.

The target is the mechanism behind `Straight Face` and `Bare Facts`, with the conceptual intelligence of `Fineprint` and more emotional aspiration.

## Pass test

A candidate advances only if a cold reader can answer both questions without a strategy presentation:

1. Why could this be skincare?
2. Why would a woman want to belong to the idea?

## Fatal gates

- Requires a definition or pronunciation lesson.
- Sounds like a slogan, campaign, clinic, education platform, or content property.
- Merely combines a skincare word with a positive adjective.
- Depends on shame, concealment, perfection, youth recovery, or fighting age.
- Contradicts the act of improving skin.
- Too close to an operating beauty or skincare name after preliminary research.
- Needs visual styling or explanatory copy to feel interesting.

## Sub-lanes inside the single mechanism

These are search angles, not separate naming strategies:

- **Face and looking idioms:** expressions where appearance and forward emotion coexist.
- **Skin-function human meaning:** resilience, composure, balance, boundaries, vitality, or form with both biological and personal readings.
- **Clarity with consequence:** truth or proof language that also implies a desirable skin outcome or confident state.
- **Self-possession:** expressions that communicate improvement on one's own terms while remaining naturally connected to skincare.

## Founder presentation

Present no more than eight names, naked and evenly formatted. Do not include a candidate merely to represent a sub-lane.
