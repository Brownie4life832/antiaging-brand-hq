# Constrained founder set

Date: 2026-07-19

## Naming thesis

High-performance skincare that tells the truth about ingredients, evidence, and aging—and treats women as informed adults, not problems to fix.

## Founder-facing set

1. True to Form
2. Open Face
3. Proof of Age
4. A Fine Age

This round is intentionally short. Every name must create an immediate connection between at least two parts of the brand idea without depending on a written rationale.

## Preliminary screen notes

This is a knockout screen, not trademark clearance or legal advice.

- **True to Form:** Unrelated exact federal records surfaced outside beauty. An older discontinued Butter London product used “True To Form” as a product name. Retained for founder reaction, with elevated naming-risk caution.
- **Open Face:** An unrelated exact federal record surfaced in restaurant services. No exact beauty brand was found in the preliminary web screen.
- **Proof of Age:** No exact federal hit or exact beauty brand was found in the preliminary screen.
- **A Fine Age:** No exact federal hit or exact beauty brand was found in the preliminary screen.

## Removed at the private gate

- **Self Evident:** Too dependent on strategy language; insufficiently skincare-specific.
- **Formyla:** Too close to a live FORMUL-A Class 003 record and an active anti-aging skincare product using “Formul-A.”

## Founder reactions

1. **True to Form:** D
2. **Open Face:** I
3. **Proof of Age:** D
4. **A Fine Age:** D

## Round result

- Founder intrigue: **Open Face**
- Disposition after deeper screen: **removed**
- Reason: exact active skincare/cosmetics brands were found at openface.io and openface.me.
- Productive mechanism: a familiar expression with an immediate literal face/skin meaning and a second meaning tied to openness, truth, or attitude.
