# Varied science-construction founder set

## Brief correction

This round does not extend the `-istry` family or keep recombining formula/dermis with truth/evidence. It explores the same underlying mechanism as Formyla, Biovidence, BYOMA, and Biossance across different scientific source words:

- a recognizable scientific idea;
- one controlled spelling change or semantic splice;
- pronounceable without explanation;
- enough transformation to feel like a brand rather than a technical term.

## Preliminary screening notes

- Local federal screening used normalized exact and edit-distance-one knockout checks against data current through 2026-07-06. It is not legal clearance.
- Molequence, Assayance, Cytovera, Efficence, and Enzyence had no exact or edit-distance-one federal knockout in that first pass.
- Epidence, Cytovera, Dermetric, Molevia, and Covalera produced adjacent beauty, skin-health, or cosmetics uses during the broader screen and were removed from the founder field.
- The field intentionally retains some exploratory names with non-skincare or geographically remote uses because this stage is testing founder taste, not producing a clearance shortlist.
