## Why the gate is deliberately small

Cycle 1 generated 427 raw names across the broad pool and a final semantic-hinge pass, representing 379 normalized unique forms. One hundred twenty names received a local U.S. federal knockout screen. Most of the technically quieter names still failed the founder's creative controls: they were forced `skin-` constructions, opaque abbreviations, generic phrases, or names whose relevance existed only in the explanation.

The five names above are the only forms worth using for founder learning. They are shown without sales copy because the naked name must do the work.

## What each result would change

- If one or more names create real pull, Cycle 2 will develop only that naming mechanism.
- If the idea is useful but every expression is wrong, Cycle 2 will retain the semantic hinge and change the verbal form.
- If all five are immediate rejections, Cycle 1 records `none`; Cycle 2 will abandon honesty/proof phrases and seek a different source of excitement.
