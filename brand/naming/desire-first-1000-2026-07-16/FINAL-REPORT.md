# Desire-first anti-aging brand naming run

**Completed:** July 16, 2026

## The outcome

This run produced **2,846 normalized-unique raw candidates**, then selected exactly **1,000 project-fresh names** across ten blind briefs. Skincare was revealed only after the names were formed. All 1,000 were creatively scored and screened against a local USPTO-derived federal corpus; the strongest 100 non-red candidates then received one exact-name web search apiece.

The practical handoff is:

- `finalists-20.csv`: the most interesting conversation set
- `finalists-50.csv`: the broader curated finalist set
- `marketplace-search-results.csv`: the 100 exact-name web checks and evidence links
- `top-200-non-red.csv`: the non-red consideration pool
- `all-1000-screen-aware-ranked.csv`: every rated name, with federal screen evidence

## The Placek-style method stack

All seventeen moves were distributed across the ten worlds rather than applied as one homogeneous word blender:

1. Intangible-to-tangible
2. Benefit ladder
3. Future reframe
4. Anti-descriptor
5. Win-by-contrast
6. Audacity / worldview
7. Deconstruct / reconstruct
8. Portmanteau
9. Morpheme engine
10. Scientific borrowing
11. Sound-first coinage
12. Structural / orthographic devices
13. Compounds
14. Real-word metaphor / eponym
15. World-language hunt
16. Far-field source raids
17. Decoy briefs

The key process intervention was the **decoy brief**: generators were naming fashion houses, private rooms, heirlooms, characters, gestures, instruments, and cultural objects—not skincare. Only after the 1,000-name set existed did the review ask whether a name could credibly carry high-performance skincare, confidence, age-positive control, and responsive expertise.

## Reading the labels correctly

- **Red / direct conflict** means eliminate from this working set.
- **Amber or trademark/product caution** means visible relevant risk; counsel should inspect it before enthusiasm grows.
- **Yellow or active other use** means a collision exists but was not an obvious same-category knockout in this basic screen.
- **Provisional pass / no obvious exact** means only that this limited screen did not expose an immediate issue. It does not establish availability, registrability, priority, common-law freedom, domain availability, or international clearance.

## My read

The most productive new territories are **Cultural Mononym** and **Beautiful Mischief**. Ricercar, Partimento, Fascicle, Better Trouble, Velvet Diversion, Polite Mischief, and Tender Detour feel less like skincare naming exercises and more like brands with an independent point of view. The strongest conventional-house options are Anvorel, Calvere, Corvenne, Brivune, Ferrore, and Elvenne; they are easier to merchandise but less conceptually surprising.

## Legal caveat

This is a preliminary naming screen, not legal advice or trademark clearance. Before adoption, trademark counsel should run a comprehensive search covering federal and state records, common-law use, phonetic and conceptual similarity, relevant goods/services, domains and social handles, and priority markets outside the United States.
