# Desire-first 1,000-name generation framework

## Locked premise

The previous exercise overproduced intelligent strategy statements. This run names an object of desire first and allows evidence, technology, confidence, and age-positive behavior to live in the brand story.

Generation is blind to skincare. Category review occurs only after a net-new 1,000-name pool exists.

## Placek method coverage

Across the ten passes the generator must use all seventeen moves: intangible-to-tangible; benefit ladder; future reframe; anti-descriptor; win-by-contrast; audacity/worldview; deconstruct/reconstruct; portmanteau; morpheme engine; scientific borrowing; sound-first coinage; structural/orthographic devices; compounds; real-word metaphor/eponym; world-language hunt; far-field source raids; and decoy briefs.

## Funnel

1. Generate at least 1,400 raw forms with no creative evaluation.
2. Normalize and remove internal duplicates.
3. Remove every exact collision with the retained project-history corpus and the direction-probe names already shown in conversation.
4. Apply only pronunciation, spelling, and prohibited-language gates.
5. Replenish until the allocation above yields exactly 1,000 project-fresh names.
6. Reveal skincare and score all 1,000 for desire, distinctiveness, fluency, stretch, bottle presence, and one-sentence story ignition.
7. Run the local federal knockout over all 1,000.
8. Remove live exact/relevant red results; retain amber/yellow visibility.
9. Curate a top 200 non-red pool, marketplace-check the strongest 100, and rank a final 50 and top 20.

## Assumptions

- Approximately 70% gender-neutral and 30% subtly feminine.
- English-first pronunciation.
- One word is preferred; two words are allowed where the collision produces a genuinely new image.
- No name needs to describe skincare, technology, aging, ingredients, or proof.
- A high creative score is not trademark clearance.
